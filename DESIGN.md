# Interface direction

This is a study workbench for CAS Exam 5, not a marketing page. The visual language should come from exam papers, calculation tables, chapter references, and scored work.

- Put real study content and the next useful action first. Home should show progress and clear routes into questions, quizzes, and retries.
- Use restrained ink, paper, and muted green colors. Keep surfaces flat, with borders doing most of the separation.
- Use a small radius scale and minimal shadows. Avoid decorative gradients, glowing elements, floating cards, and hover movement.
- Keep typography readable during long question sessions. Question text, tables, answer fields, and solutions take priority over labels and decoration.
- Write specific copy tied to the exam and the user's workflow. Remove helper text once the control is self-explanatory.
- Preserve clear focus states, saved work, empty states, and narrow-screen layouts. A distinctive look cannot come at the expense of usability.
